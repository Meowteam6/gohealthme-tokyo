## Pending: the device loop (needs a real iPhone and Watch)

Run `cd app && node --env-file=.env.local scripts/apple-device-qa.mjs 0xWALLET 8` while the tester walks it. Each line flips from WAIT to PASS as the server sees it.

1. Install GoHealthMe from TestFlight (Founders group, no review needed).
2. On the phone, site > step 4 > Pair my Apple Watch > Open the GoHealthMe app > Pair > Allow. Target: four taps, two of them Apple's.
3. App shows "Synced N days for 0x...". Site flips to "Apple Watch is paired" with no refresh.
4. Join pool 8 (one workout) or pool 7 (sleep 7 hours, Watch worn to bed).
5. Do the thing. Background delivery posts the day; the verdict shows on the challenge page; the sweep records it.
6. Negative paths: deny the Health sheet (copy says it is inconclusive, retry offered); pair a second phone (first revoked); expired code (one tap for a new one).

Known limit of the QA script: steps 1 and 2 read the pairing records from KV, which the local env does not share with prod; steps 3, 4 and 6 read prod's database and chain and are the proof.
