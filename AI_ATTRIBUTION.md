# AI Attribution

This project uses AI-assisted development (Claude Code). Per ETHGlobal rules, AI-assisted files are listed here and kept current through each event.

The file spans four builds of one repo lineage: ETHGlobal New York 2026 (Jun 12-14, the rows at the top of the table), the Circle Agentic Economy build (Aug 2026), the Base V3 pilot (Aug to Sep 2026), and ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 (Sep 25-27, the section directly below and every table row marked "ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026"). Every row was reviewed by the team (Andre Chuabio, Nikki Hu).

## ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 (2026-09-25 21:00 JST to 2026-09-27 09:00 JST)

Everything after commit `a86387d` on `main` (321 commits at `90231c2`) was written during the event. The honest split:

**AI-generated, human-reviewed (Claude Code).** Most of the code and most of the copy. Each lane (foundation, contracts, world-idkit, world-agents, ens, intercepta, ux, then the fix, sensors, money, design and docs lanes) was run by a Claude Code session from a written lane contract (`docs/LANES.md`) or a spec, test-first where the row says so, in a worktree, then reviewed and merged. That includes the World, ENS and Intercepta integrations, the recorded-miss rule, the gas drip, the Night Shift components, the state gallery, the privacy and terms rewrite, the lane docs, `docs/EVALUATE.md`, `docs/QA-2026-09-26.md`, the README continuity section and this file. Reviews were also AI-run (a QA sweep of the merged build, adversarial reviewers on the miss rule, screenshot critics on the redesign); their findings were fixed by AI sessions and the fixes were reviewed by Andre.

**Commit trailers.** 278 of the 321 event commits carry a `Co-Authored-By` trailer naming the Claude model that drove the session (252 Claude Opus 5.5, 26 Claude Fable 5.1); the tool adds the trailer and the commit author is the human whose session ran it. The remaining 43 carry none.

**Human decisions (Andre Chuabio, with Nikki Hu on product and compliance).**
- Product direction: the Continuity thesis (V4 stays GoHealthMe, reorganised around the player's run), the three-screen loop with character creation once, World on both tracks, Intercepta as the third pick, and the hard rule that V4 must work for real beta users with no mock reachable in production.
- Design: three directions were built as real rendered mocks and judged; Andre rejected SPOTTER's Riverbank and chose Night Shift, with the light theme deferred (decisions log in `docs/DESIGN.md`). "Dare" became "challenge" on his call.
- Money-model rules: run the real commitment model, record a miss only when the wearable shows it after a grace window, never on missing data; the `MISS_RULE_FROM_POOL_ID` cutoff so earlier players keep the refund they were promised; no fee on V4; which contract gaps wait until after the event.
- World credentials: no Orb gate, any World App credential, Selfie Check dropped after it failed on his phone.
- Scope cuts: Apple Health out of the submission; the Apple pairing branch parked.
- Every deploy and promotion, every environment import, every key and account (World Developer Portal app, RP and signing key; Intercepta; Junction; WHOOP; Coinbase Developer Platform; Circle; Upstash; Supabase; Vercel), every pool seed and cancel on Base Sepolia, and the ENSv2 bootstrap on Sepolia. Sessions prepared scripts and variable names; humans ran them and held the keys.
- Nothing outward (submission text, forms, posts) is sent by a session; drafts go to an approval queue and Andre submits.

**Verified by humans on a device.**
- Character creation on Andre's phone and laptop against the live build: sign in, World ID verified with a real World App credential (Production app), `andre.gohealthme.eth` minted on ENSv2 Sepolia and resolving in the header.
- The first real join on V4 from an email wallet on Andre's phone, asserted on chain: ETH 0 to 0.000499 from the gas drip, USDC 50 to 49, pool balance 8 to 9.
- The human gate on names, driven by Andre on a preview.
- The Intercepta live-key probe and the same check against the deployed build (clean wallets score 0; four OFAC SDN addresses blocked).
- Signed-out walks of the live build were driven by sessions in a real browser, not by a human.

**Not yet exercised on a live run at submission (2026-09-27 06:00 JST).** No V4 pool had settled. The World ID payout confirmation, the Intercepta screen on a real payout and the ENS settlement receipt are covered by route and contract tests and were still waiting on the first V4 settle.
